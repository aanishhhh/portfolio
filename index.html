/about

Working across the full chip lifecycle

I'm a B.Tech Electronics Engineering (VLSI Design and Technology) student at VIT Chennai, and I've spent the last two years going deep on the parts of chip design most people only see one slice of: writing RTL and microarchitecture, proving it correct with UVM and formal verification, and carrying it through ASIC physical design to a manufacturable GDSII.

That's shown up as an FPGA-based neuromorphic accelerator at Edge Synapse, a full RTL-to-GDSII SPI tapeout flow at Maven Silicon, and independent projects like SparseFlow, a sparsity-aware neural network accelerator I took from architecture through a DRC-clean layout on my own.

Outside the lab, I spent two years running operations for VIT's Finance and Management Club — club member to Operations Director to Advisory — which is where I learned that good hardware and good process both come down to the same thing: verify everything, assume nothing.
